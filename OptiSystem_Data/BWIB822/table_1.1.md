# popular CRM metrics

## Shares

### market share

- The **percentage of a firm’s sales** to the sales of all firms in a given market


## rates 

### acquisition rate 

- The **proportion** of prospects **converted to customers**

### retention rate

- The **average likelihood** that a customer makes a **repurchase** from the focal firm in period $t$, given that this customer has purchased in the last period $t$-1

### defection rate 

- The **average likelihood** that a customer
**defects** from the focal firm in period $t$,
given that this customer has purchased in
the last period $t$-1

### win-back rate

- The **ratio of acquisition of customers** who
had been lost in an earlier period


## values

### Acquisition cost

- The acquisition **spending** of a focal firm **per
prospect acquired**


### Past customer value 

- The **gross contribution** of a customer when
**adjusted** for the time value of money

### RFM value 

#### Recency 

- Recency indicates the most recent
purchase date of a customer

#### Frequency 
- Frequency measures how often a
customer purchases from the firm


#### Monetary
- Monetary value measures the average per transaction spending of a customer
- 

### Customer lifetime value 

- the **total discounted contribution margins**
of a customer (excess of recurring
revenues over recurring costs to the focal
firm) over a specific time period

### Customer equity

- The total lifetime value of all customers of the focal firm
