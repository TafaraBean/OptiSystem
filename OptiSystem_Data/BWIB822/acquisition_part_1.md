# customer life cycle

## customer acquisition (step 1)

- more costly than customer retention


## modelling customer data 

- determine whether customers are contractual or non-contractual

## contractual customers 

- churn is observed
- switching cost determines the relationship
- concerned with expected duration of the relationship


## non contractual customers

- retail companies 
- churn is not observed
- customers split their spendings between multiple companies
- no switching costs 
- loyalty programmes are important

## concerns of companies with non-contractual customers

- the probability that a new customer will be attracted to make a purchase for the first time
- how many orders the customer will make 
- how much profit they will generate of their lifetime

## strategies to increase the acquisition rate

### increase market size 

- this is the number of potential customers
- critical driver for acquisition 
- more prospects means greater market potential
- less prospects means **no increase in market share** and detrimental to the current focus and structure.  this leads to a lower acquisition rate.
- develop new usage cases 
- target a new customer segment

### increase acquisition marketing expenditure 

- customers are usually acquired at a loss and its usually made up in the future

### acquisition pricing and promotions

#### lower prices and promotions

- almost always increases acquisition rate but affect's future prices

## Acquisition Targeting methods

### profiling 

#### proposal

- profile the existing customers
- target customers using characteristics of the current customers

#### process
- take a sample of the current customers
- append to each record of customers some demographic information or other customer characteristics
- cross-tab or cluster the customers on the characteristics to describe them or profile them
- target customers with similar profiles

#### Problems
- how do you determine which variables are important?
- we only target customers with similar profiles

- but these targets were acquired using a previous targeting method.

- profiling might miss customers who would have purchased if given the opportunity

#### solutions 

- discriminant analysis, logistic regression, decision trees etc.
- draw random samples from lists to see if those who resposnd have the same characteristics as current customers. this is like a proof-of-concept


### Random testing of prospects databases

- we get a set of lists
- from each list, draw a random sample of indiviaduals to mail
- evaluate the repsonse rate of each list 
- send larger quantity of mail to lists above a cut-off response rate 

### 2-step acquisition methods

- 1. initial non purchase communicatiobn
- 2. send offer of purchase to respondents
- used when there is lack of precision in identifying prospective customers
- this method depends on the cost structure of reaching specific audiences and the nature of targeting

### predictive modelling

- get a random sample of names from lists 
- append demographic information to names 
- take note of the respondents 
- model the response on demographics
