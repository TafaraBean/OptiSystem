## CRM (Customer relationship management)

### Customer Value (CV)

- Financial value of a customer
- no specific time duration

### Customer lifetime Value (CLV)
- net economic value of a customer
- measured over the entire lifetime with the company
- used to see how customer relationship evolves over time

### Ultimate Goal
- maximize the the CLV
