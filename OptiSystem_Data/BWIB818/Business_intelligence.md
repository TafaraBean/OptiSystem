## What is Business Intelligence (BI) and what is its primary purpose?
* Business intelligence is a term used to describe a comprehensive, cohesive, and integrated set of tools and processes.
* These tools are used to capture, collect, integrate, store, and analyze data.
* The purpose of this is to generate and present information to support business decision-making.
* Business intelligence involves capturing internal and external metadata.
* It allows a business to transform data into information and then transforming that information into knowledge.

## What framework does BI provide for a business?
BI provides a framework for:
* Collection and storing of operational data.
* Aggregation of operational data to obtain decision support data.
* Analyzing of decision support data to produce information.
* Prediction of future behaviors (which also produces information).
* Presentation of information to support business decisions.
* Making the business decisions which generate the data.
* Evaluation of business decisions which also produces data to be analyzed.

## What are the basic architectural components of BI?
* **ETL tools:** These collect, filter, integrate, and aggregate data to be stored in a data store.
* **Data store:** Optimized for decision support. Includes data warehouses and data marts. Stored in structures which are optimized for data analysis and query speed.
* **Query and reporting tools:** Perform data selection and retrieval. Access the data store and develop reports.
* **Data visualization tools:** Optimized for decision support. Presents data in a meaningful way such as summary reports, maps, graphs, and dashboards.
* **Data monitoring and alerting tools:** Real-time monitoring of business activities.
* **Data analytics:** Performs data analysis and data mining. Can be descriptive or predictive.

## What is Master Data Management (MDM)?
* An arrangement of the best managerial practices to manage data as a corporate asset is called master data management.
* The main objective is to provide a consistent and comprehensive definition of data within an organization to enable a uniform understanding of the organization's data.

## What are Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)?
* They are a special type of metric.
* They are scale-based measurements used to evaluate the company's effectiveness in reaching strategic and operational goals.

## What are the key differences between Operational Data and Decision Support (DS) data?
* Operational data and decision support data serve different purposes.
* **Operational data:**
    * Optimized to support transactions which represent daily operations.
    * Stored in highly normalized relational databases for effective update performance.
    * Usually covers a smaller timeframe.
    * Only looks at individual transactions and not their effect over time.
* **Decision Support (DS) data:**
    * Operational data is not well suited for decision making; DS data provides meaning to the operational data.
    * Optimized for query performance.
    * Stored in denormalized data marts.
    * Covers a longer timeframe.
    * Usually has 3 levels of aggregation. The summarized levels can be drilled down into more atomic components, and can also be rolled up from the atomic components (lower levels) to a more summarized level (higher levels).
* Operational data and DS data differ in terms of time span, granularity, and dimensionality.
* Data analysts are more focused on different dimensions of data and how data relates over these different dimensions.

## How can we summarize Business Intelligence?
* BI is an umbrella term which refers to the tools, tasks, and processes for BI. 
* The main goal is to support decision making.
