# The example we should follow.

We should **follow GOD's example**. This means **walking in love** and being a **fragrant sacrifice** unto the Lord.

Following God's example also means having the same dislike for sin (sexual immorality, debauchery, greed etc.). We are mandated to remove every kind of impurity from ourselves. In the place of all things that God does not like, we should fill it with thanksgiving. You cannot have an inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God while you carry impurity. 

# We should seek that which pleases the Lord.

We need to **find** out what pleases the Lord. this insinuates that it won't be clear to the eye initially but will be revealed to you.

We were once in the darkness, but that darkness cannot exist in his light. We should not sit idly by when deeds of darkness are done but we should expose them, for everything in the light must be visible, **AND ALL THAT IS ILLUMINATED BECOMES A LIGHT**. We are illuminated by the Lord, hence we are light and live blamelessly, exposing the dark deeds and giving no space for it. 


# Be careful how you live. 

We should live not as unwise but as wise. We must make the most of every opportunity and **speak to one another in psalms, hymns and songs from the spirit**. Make music from the heart to the Lord and give thanks for EVERYTHING.


# Instructions for the household

submitting to one another is a way to revere christ. Wives should submit to their husbands the same way they would submit to Christ - The same way the church submits to Christ. 

Husbands should Love their wives and give themselves up for her in the same way Christ gave himself up for us, cleansing us and removing blemishes. 

To love your wife is to also love yourself. "He who Loves his wife loves himself". Christ loves his body (us as the church).
